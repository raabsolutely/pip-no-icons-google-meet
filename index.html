PiP No Icons Google Meet GitHub

Meet's floating window, without the buttons.

A small Chrome extension that strips Google Meet's picture-in-picture window down to the people in your call. Shrink it to a strip and keep working.

Hover or tap the window to bring the controls back.

Download the extension Free, two small files, for Chrome on Mac and Windows.

Main use case

Position the participant strip just underneath your camera bezel. When you speak, you naturally look at their faces, and that means looking into the camera. The people in the call see you making eye contact.

What changes

Hidden

  • The control bar, which comes back when you hover the window
  • Names, which come back when you hover a face
  • Mic icons and tile menus
  • The Gemini star

Unchanged

  • The video and Meet's highlight on whoever is speaking
  • Meet's own buttons, which still work as normal
  • The main Meet window

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it somewhere it can stay, such as your Documents folder. Chrome loads the extension from this folder, so don't delete it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the pip-no-icons-google-meet folder.

If you use several Chrome profiles, install it in the one your Meet app opens with.

Use

  1. In a call, open More options (the three dots) and choose Open picture-in-picture mode.
  2. Drag the window's edge until it's a strip, and move it just under your camera.
  3. Hover the window when you need to mute, turn off your camera, or leave.

Good to know

  • It only changes how the floating window looks. It reads no data and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Chrome's own title bar belongs to the browser and stays.
  • When Google updates Meet, hidden parts can reappear. Download the latest zip, replace your folder, and click reload in chrome://extensions.
  • Company-managed Chrome may block Developer mode. If Load unpacked is missing, that's why.
